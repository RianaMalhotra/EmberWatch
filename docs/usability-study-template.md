# EmberWatch Explore usability study (ECE444 PRA4)

**User story:** As a visitor, I want to locate a monitored area, inspect its risk assessment and satellite detections, and understand whether the information is current.

**Task prompt (read verbatim):** "You are checking wildfire conditions near a Canadian city. Find an area with elevated modeled risk, inspect the available observations, and tell me how confident you are in what the map shows."

**Success criteria:** Participant locates a monitored area, distinguishes risk score from satellite confidence, finds an observation timestamp, and recognizes that EmberWatch is not an emergency alert service without hints.

Test with two real participants outside the project team, separately. Use the same original interface for both; do not revise it until both initial tests are complete. Ask them to think aloud. Do not coach.
